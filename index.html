Subscriptions

CatRoutine 2.0 offers monthly and yearly subscriptions. An active subscription or eligible free trial enables care creation and completion, health tracking and other Premium features for up to three cats. For new users without active access, existing records remain readable and deletable, while Premium actions are locked. Closing an offer does not activate Premium.

A free trial may be offered with the yearly plan when Apple determines you are eligible. The purchase screen shows the actual trial duration, price, billing period and renewal terms before you confirm. Unless cancelled, the trial becomes a paid subscription and subscriptions renew automatically under the terms displayed by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

If you cancel a free trial, or a trial ends without a payment, CatRoutine may offer a one-time discounted first year of the yearly plan. The app shows the first-year price, the regular yearly price it renews at, and the last day of the offer. If you accept while your free trial is still running, your remaining free days continue and the discounted first year starts, and is charged, when the trial ends. After the first year the subscription renews at the regular yearly price unless you cancel. The offer is purchased and billed by Apple like any other subscription.

New lifetime purchases are no longer offered. Previously purchased lifetime access and active subscriptions remain eligible for restoration through the same Apple account. Existing local basic-access rights are retained on upgrade; they do not grant all new Premium features. Contact support if your earlier access is not recognised.
